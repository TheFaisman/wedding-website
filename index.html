Q & A

For friends and family who have questions.

What's the RSVP deadline?

The deadline to RSVP is on September 15th.

Are kids welcome?

In an effort to keep things running smoothly and beautifully, we kindly request that kids remain at home.

Where can I park?

Parking won't be an issue as there's ample parking at the venue.

Is this wedding mixed or segregated?

The wedding will be mixed throughout.

Can I bring any additional guests?

As this is a seated dinner, we must unfortunately be strict with guest count and seating.

If your invitation includes room for an additional guest, please note that you should RSVP on their behalf and provide their full name for use in their namecard.

Is the wedding indoors or outdoors?

The wedding is indoors, although there's also a patio guests can sit at.

Is smoking allowed?

Smoking is strictly prohibited, even on the outside patio.

Can I make changes to my RSVP submission?

Yes, changes can be made up until the deadline.
